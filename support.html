Help with features

This week's plan. On Home, three drills for the week picked from your own games and drills, with the reason for each. A new plan arrives every Monday.

Import a game from a box score. For softball, baseball and basketball: export the box score as a PDF from your scorekeeping app (for example GameChanger), then on Games tap Import a game. The file is read on your phone and never uploaded, and only your player's own line is kept. DrillStreak is not affiliated with or endorsed by GameChanger.

Reminders. Optional and off until you turn them on, on the Account screen: a weekly plan nudge, off-day nudges, and a heads-up before a free trial ends. They live on your phone and never include names or scores.

Club code. If your club gave you a code, enter it on Account. You choose whether to share; the club sees only combined numbers for its families (groups under 5 are hidden), never your name or your child's. You can leave the club any time on Account.

Flag football. Flag Football is its own sport with its own drills and game tracker. Switch to it with the sport chip at the top of any screen.

Membership. Parent is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial for new members, and covers every player on your account in both apps. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Delete your account

DrillStreak and DrillStreak IQ share one account, so deleting it removes it from both apps. The fastest way: in either app, open the Account screen and tap Delete account.

Or, to delete your DrillStreak account and all associated data by email, email support@drillstreak.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." We'll confirm your identity and delete your account within a reasonable time.

What's deleted: your account, player profiles, drill completions, badges, team memberships and messages, display name, and in DrillStreak IQ, your lesson completions and any lessons you assigned as a coach.

What's retained, and why: subscription/payment records are kept as required for legal and accounting purposes — see our Privacy Policy for details.

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific data — a player profile, custom drill, team, logged completion, or challenge can each be deleted directly within the app.
